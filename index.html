About me

Hi, I'm Sakshi, a graphic designer from Lucknow with a love for digital art, illustration and video editing.

I work in Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects and Premiere Pro, and I enjoy turning an idea into a complete brand look, from logo to packaging to motion. I'm still growing as a designer, and every project is a chance to learn a new technique.

I'm looking to work on real projects, learn from experienced people and build my career in design.
